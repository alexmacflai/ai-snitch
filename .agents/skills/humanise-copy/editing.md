# Copy editing

Used by [humanise-copy](SKILL.md). Keep the original and two intermediate versions or attributable diffs. The source of voice is the person's supplied writing and the project's approved copy guidance, not an invented personality profile.

## Broad-pattern pass

Read for meaning, audience and intended action. Remove inflated claims, vague abstractions, unsupported attribution, unnecessary setup and repetitions that obscure the point. Preserve specifics, uncertainty and strong lines. Ask or flag when a precise replacement needs an unknown fact; never invent numbers or experiences to make prose vivid.

## Focused-pattern pass

Inspect binary contrasts, throat-clearing openers, fake-insight setups, superficial explanatory phrases, repetitive sentence shapes, artificial rhythm and redundant endings. Edit only patterns weakening this text. Do not mechanically ban words, force short sentences or remove deliberate humour. Preserve blunt language and uneven cadence when they belong to the writer.

After each pass, identify changes separately. Check names, numbers, claims, jokes, uncertainty, action semantics and promises against the original. “Archive” must not become “delete”; an error message must not promise recovery the product cannot provide. For microcopy, concision and clarity matter more than adding personality.

Use a small relevant selection of approved writing samples from the project's existing copywriting area; link rather than duplicate them in generic skills. Offer a human read-aloud/edit of prominent lines and incorporate the person's changes. Completion of agent editing does not imply that human review happened.

## Source assessment

[Humanizer](https://github.com/softaworks/agent-toolkit/blob/main/skills/humanizer/SKILL.md) covers broad patterns but includes encouragement to inject personality. [No AI Slop](https://github.com/petergyang/no-ai-slop/blob/main/skills/no-ai-slop/SKILL.md) emphasises minimum edits and preserving voice, alongside word/pattern bans. [Mika Reyes's workflow](https://mikareyes.com/ai/how-to-humanize-ai-text) separates the passes and uses actual writing samples.

This procedure uses independently written guidance informed by those approaches. It installs neither external skill, copies no checklist wholesale and rejects invented personality and blanket bans. Further reuse requires checking current instructions and licensing. Named patterns are editorial observations, not evidence of AI authorship.
