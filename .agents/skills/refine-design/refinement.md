# Design refinement

Used by [refine-design](SKILL.md). Compare each pass with the selected direction and user task, retaining before/intermediate/after versions so changes remain attributable.

Subtraction examines redundant explanation, competing emphasis, unnecessary containers, controls and effects. Remove what weakens the experience; preserve information needed for comprehension, error recovery and action consequences.

Visual-pattern review asks whether a treatment has a reason here. A repeated feature-card layout, glow, gradient or decorative graphic may be appropriate. Name the actual composition or purpose problem and the smallest effective correction; do not replace one familiar style with another merely to avoid a pattern.

Copy review uses [humanise-copy](../humanise-copy/SKILL.md). Keep functional promises, labels, facts and intended tone aligned with behaviour. Expose changes by pass. A personal read-aloud checkpoint is offered for important lines, not claimed on the person's behalf.

Detail work checks hierarchy, typography, spacing, assets and applicable responsive/interactive states. Inspect [media](../develop-design/media.md) where motion is present. Revisit earlier stages when a major unresolved choice prevents local polish.

Verification reports what was actually inspected, exercised or tested and at what fidelity. Screenshots can support visual judgements; they cannot establish keyboard behaviour, accessibility compliance or production readiness. Use [verify-work](../verify-work/SKILL.md). Finish at the agreed endpoint without publishing or turning every experiment into a permanent design rule.
