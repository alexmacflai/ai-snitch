# Develop a design

Turns a promising direction into a convincing rendered experience through purposeful creative interventions.

## When to use

- Developing a selected direction or an authorised prototype.
- Not for selecting a direction by default or silently implementing a production feature.

## Inputs

- The brief, constraints, references, selected direction, artefacts and authorised endpoint.

## Steps

1. Read the shared [workflow](../design-with-ai/workflow.md). Recover the brief and authority to prototype; selection alone does not authorise building. If direction is unresolved, return to [ideate-design](../ideate-design/SKILL.md).
2. Make the initial prototype at the agreed fidelity, preserving fixed decisions. State the creative question for this iteration. Use [prompting](../design-with-ai/prompting.md) when delegating or generating.
3. Capture and inspect the rendered result at relevant sizes and states. Never use source inspection as visual evidence. Save the inspected evidence and retain earlier versions.
4. Apply the [independent critique procedure](critique.md). Evaluate suggestions against the brief, make consequential changes, and capture again. Keep critic inputs independent of implementation history.
5. Read [imagery and motion](media.md) when another medium could strengthen the direction. Consider imagery, video and code-based motion; record the choice even when no added media is useful. Inspect transitions in context.
6. After at most two autonomous critique-and-change rounds per batch, assess convergence. Continue only for material progress within scope; ask for unresolved taste decisions, stop on repeated/conflicting feedback, or return to ideation if the concept fails.
7. Record the question, intervention, evidence and decision for each significant iteration. When major choices are resolved, hand to [refine-design](../refine-design/SKILL.md) if authorised.

## Done when

- The rendered experience expresses the selected identity and major composition and interaction choices are resolved.
- Evidence, remaining local issues, critique limitations and the next step are recorded.
