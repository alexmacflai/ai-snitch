# Imagery and motion

Used by [develop-design](SKILL.md) and [refine-design](../refine-design/SKILL.md). Consider media as a creative intervention: what can material, atmosphere, physical behaviour or a change of state contribute to this direction? Record the decision, including a justified choice to add nothing.

Images can establish a distinctive subject, texture or spatial treatment. Generate or source assets for their actual placement; inspect framing, scale, legibility, palette and integration in the rendered design. Do not add decorative assets merely to satisfy a technique list.

For transitions, define start/end states, user trigger, purpose, continuity, duration, interruption and reversal. Choose code-based motion for behaviour requiring live interaction or adaptable state; consider video for expressive physical or cinematic sequences. Video depicts frames rather than dynamic application state: confirm its integration does not obscure controls or misrepresent what happened.

Where appropriate, seed a subsequent clip from the previous final frame and inspect seams, cropping, transparency or background removal. Check responsive fit, loading, scrubbing and playback failure. Inspect key frames and the running sequence; endpoint stills alone cannot prove continuity. Provide reduced-motion behaviour that preserves the action and destination.

Discover available capabilities before promising generation. Use the environment's relevant tool guidance. Surface unavailable tools, external costs or unmet authorisation before dependent work; never request secrets in product code or claim an asset was generated when only a prompt exists. Use an authorised alternative or record a blocker. Keep prompts, source assets, iterations and inspected evidence linked in the exploration record.
