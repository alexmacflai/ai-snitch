# Product

AI Snitch will be a public, growing library of stylistic habits people associate with AI-generated writing. Each entry explains the habit, shows an illustrative example, cites evidence and makes uncertainty visible. The name invites a cheeky, conspiratorial voice; the factual content must earn trust.

## Confirmed intent

Document patterns, their reported history and changing relevance. Keep insufficiently supported candidates out of the public library. Eventually export a reusable Markdown/rules/skill library for other projects, and use it to review this project's own copy. All services and operations must cost zero money. GitHub Pages is the likely initial host, subject to the researched recommendation.

The project is now published in the public [GitHub repository](https://github.com/alexmacflai/ai-snitch). Repository publication is complete; site deployment and a live scouting schedule remain separate proposed work. Repository details are recorded in [setup](../setup.md).

## Audience assumptions

Writers, designers, editors and developers are plausible readers; these are working audience hypotheses, without interviews or validated demand. English-language coverage is the recommended initial scope. There is no evidence yet for usage frequency or measurable impact.

## Proposed journeys

1. A curious reader browses a pattern, sees an invented example clearly labelled, checks the sources and understands its limits.
2. An editor searches by phrase or category, reads when the advice applies and exports the current writing guidance.
3. A maintainer reviews scouting leads, accepts or rejects evidence, updates an entry and records the reason for any status change.
4. An integrating project pins a versioned rules release, reviews flagged passages and consciously adopts or ignores each suggestion.

## Principles

- Explain the association and its evidence. A stylistic match never establishes authorship.
- Make uncertainty visible beside the claim, including unknown dates and limited study settings.
- Preserve history when evidence changes; retirement and reactivation require reasons.
- Keep humorous framing separate from factual conclusions.
- Prefer a small maintainable system with no required paid service.

## Boundaries

No detector score, accusations against named writers, promised evasion of detectors or blanket ban on normal punctuation. No accounts, database, paid domain, analytics service or live AI endpoint is needed for the initial site. Trends and audience needs remain unvalidated.
